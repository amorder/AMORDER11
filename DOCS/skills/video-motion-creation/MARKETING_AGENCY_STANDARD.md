# AMORDER marketing-agency Reel standard

This is the default release standard for AMORDER promotional and educational Reels. A technically valid MP4 is not necessarily a finished video.

## Creative bar

For AMORDER YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, one shared 9:16 master must be strictly under 160 seconds (2:40). The preferred story target is 105–145 seconds where it is narratively justified; never pad or rush narration to hit a number. Deliver 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, burned English captions and an SRT sidecar.

The goal is a directed short-form film: a strong first-second visual hook, clear narrative progression, authentic footage or purposeful original animation, intentional sound, carefully art-directed graphics and one useful action at the end.

A Reel must not be a slideshow with slow zooms, Ken Burns movement, static illustration cards or animated text over repeated stills. Typography-led motion graphics are valid only when the brief specifically calls for that format and the design itself carries the story.

## Coverage targets

Unless the brief documents a meaningful exception:

- Five or more distinct moving-footage/original-animation setups, with Blender keyframed camera and object motion for AMORDER 3D scenes.
- Eight or more distinct visual assets across footage, 2D/3D animation, detail, environment, interface or data graphics.
- At least 60% of picture time from rights-cleared moving footage or original 2D/3D sequences with genuine object/camera animation.
- A meaningful visual/action hook in the first 1.5 seconds.
- Clear progression: hook → context → explanation/proof → payoff → CTA.
- No clip reuse simply to extend runtime.
- Human-recorded narration preferred; approved local TTS only after full-script audition.
- Music and designed sound support the narration rather than masking weak editing.
- Brand-consistent typography, readable English captions, safe margins and a useful CTA.
- Source URL, creator, license evidence, acquisition date and intended-use note for each external asset.
- A factual source ledger and documented reconstruction/AI labels wherever relevant.

Do not fake compliance with the targets by counting title cards, still-image zooms or repeated crops as moving footage. For an editorially valid exception, explain the reason in the brief and obtain human review.

## 100-point review score

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Moving footage / distinct setup coverage | 25 |
| Story, opening hook and pacing | 15 |
| Cinematography, continuity and visual craft | 15 |
| Narration, music and sound design | 15 |
| Brand identity, typography and accessibility | 10 |
| Claims, sources and disclosures | 10 |
| Export integrity and platform preview | 10 |

Release target: 85/100 or higher based on recorded automated evidence, with all critical rights/factual/disclosure and media-integrity checks passed and the owner's standing auto-publishing authorization active. A missing critical item means HOLD regardless of score. Do not claim human creative approval when none was recorded.

## Revision loop

1. Render a review draft.
2. Generate a contact sheet and shot/source manifest.
3. Run the technical and coverage validator.
4. Rank failures by viewer impact: weak hook, missing movement, repeated shots, unnatural narration, unreadable captions, bad audio, unsupported claim or unclear CTA.
5. Revise the story, shot list, scenes or edit—not just add filters and transitions.
6. Render again and record concrete revision decisions.
7. Run the complete automated decode, audio, caption, visual-motion, frame integrity, source/rights and disclosure checks against the exact export.
8. Re-run the release gate if any later edit changes that export.

## AMORDER-specific rules

- English only by default, for a global audience.
- Use clear, natural, composed language. Avoid hype and financial promises.
- No-trade days may be covered as honest process stories.
- Paper-trading disclosures must state that activity is simulated, not live-money performance, not investment advice and not a promise of returns.
- Do not disclose private trade records, symbols, broker screens, fills, positions or P&L.
- Only the existing AMORDER auto-publishing workflow can queue content after all automated release checks pass under the owner's standing authorization. Verify each platform receipt and permalink before marking it published.
