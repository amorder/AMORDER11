# AMORDER core video production skill

## Mission

Produce original, evidence-led videos with the craft ambition of premium international factual and branded programming. The creative signature is minimal, modern, realistic, immersive, human and cinematic. Every shot, graphic, sound and line of narration must serve the story.

“Premium” is a creative target, not a claim of platform endorsement, buyer approval, certification or guaranteed commercial acceptance.

## Required operating rules

1. Write the story and define the viewer takeaway before creating visuals.
2. Research factual claims from primary sources and corroborate important or contested claims.
3. Keep a claim ledger for material facts, statistics, quotes, dates, forecasts and interpretations.
4. Keep a timecoded shot list, visual bible, asset manifest and rights evidence.
5. Use original footage, licensed footage, purposeful Blender scenes and carefully designed motion. Never pad runtime with repeated stills or arbitrary zooms.
6. Audition the full narration and music mix. Natural English delivery is required.
7. Label simulations, reconstructions and generated visuals wherever a viewer could mistake them for literal evidence.
8. Run technical validation and a complete human watch/listen review on the exact release export.
9. Publish only through the approved AMORDER workflow after required editorial and rights approval.
10. Report every stage truthfully. A render is not the same as a reviewed, approved or published video.

## AMORDER content limits

AMORDER11 is a public content repository, not the trading system or private research database. Do not expose broker payloads, credentials, order/account identifiers, private scans, symbol-level research, positions, fills, balances, private journals, trade-by-trade details or private performance figures.

Content may discuss general market education, research discipline, data quality, paper trading and risk management. Do not publish buy/sell calls, price targets, fabricated metrics or return promises. If describing simulated activity, disclose that it is paper trading and not live-money performance or investment advice.

A no-trade day is valid editorial material. Explain patience, process discipline or why criteria were not met using approved public information. Never invent a trade or imply that a simulated trade happened when it did not.

## Default creative style

- Language: English throughout, including voiceover, overlays, captions, metadata and subtitles.
- Audience: global; avoid unexplained local slang and assumptions.
- Tone: calm, articulate, credible, thoughtful and conversational. Prefer precise explanations over dramatic claims.
- Picture: cinematic realism, intentional composition, motivated light, detailed materials and restrained colour.
- Motion: smooth camera/object animation with believable acceleration and deceleration. Every move must have a purpose.
- Graphics: minimal, data-accurate, readable on a phone, with clear source/date/unit context.
- Sound: clear narration, carefully balanced music and designed ambience; no generic drone to disguise weak editing.
- Accessibility: proofread subtitles, readable contrast, safe margins and clear pronunciation.

## Production tools

Use the current AMORDER tools first: Blender scene generation, FFmpeg/ffprobe finishing and validation, Python/Pillow graphics and the existing short/episode renderers. Optional local AI models may assist with ideation, assets, speech or transcription only after the exact model revision, license, hardware needs and output rights are reviewed. Free-to-download does not automatically mean commercially reusable.

## Release states

Use explicit states such as DRAFT, RESEARCHED, SCRIPT_APPROVED, STORYBOARDED, ASSETS_CLEARED, RENDERED, TECH_QA_PASSED, HUMAN_APPROVED, QUEUED, PUBLISHED, HOLD and FAILED. Record evidence for every transition. Never turn an error, ambiguous upload or missing review into a successful status.
