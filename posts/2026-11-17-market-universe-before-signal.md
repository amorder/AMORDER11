## Platform copy

**Instagram caption**

A market scan is only as reliable as the universe behind it. Start by checking what each instrument is, whether it is tradable through the intended route, whether its quote and history are usable, and which markets are genuinely connected. Different products can behave differently: the SEC notes that leveraged and inverse ETFs are built around daily objectives, so longer-period results can diverge from the underlying benchmark.

A clean research universe does not predict returns or create a recommendation. It makes the evidence easier to interpret.
